# Launch Video Plan: Meadow 🌸

> **Creative Angle**: The anti-Zoom hangout. A sun-drenched 3D meadow with a tactile retro cassette deck, crystal-clear real-time voice chat, and synchronized parlor games (Snakes & Ladders + Ludo Classic) played seamlessly without leaving the view.

---

## 1. Specifications

- **Format**: Landscape 16:9 (`1920 × 1080`), 30 frames per second
- **Duration**: 20.0 seconds (600 frames total)
- **Tone**: `default` (Punchy, cozy, playful, tactile)
- **Soundtrack**: Lo-fi chillhop beat (90 BPM) composed of Rhodes electric piano chords, vinyl crackle, tape deck tactile mechanical clicks, and synchronized in-game SFX (dice rolls, pawn step pops, ladder climb chimes, emoji bursts).

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Real Project Assets

- **Color Palette**:
  - Meadow Greens: `#22c55e`, `#15803d`, `#86efac`
  - Sky & Dandelions: `#fef08a`, `#e0f2fe`, `#38bdf8`
  - Cassette Deck: Tactile dark matte `#18181b`, brushed metal, amber vu-meters `#f59e0b`
  - Parlor Boards: Authentic ivory `#f5f0e8`, wooden border `#8b4513`, player quadrant gradients (Ruby Red `#ef4444`, Emerald Green `#10b981`, Sapphire Blue `#3b82f6`, Sunflower Yellow `#eab308`)
- **Typography**: Inter & system sans-serif, monospaced tactile indicators
- **Real Components Reused**:
  - 3D Meadow canvas with floating flowers and dandelion pollen
  - Tactile cassette deck with turning spools and playback timeline
  - Multi-user Voice Chat indicator pills
  - Authentic Snakes & Ladders 100-cell checkered grid with 3D player pawns
  - Authentic Ludo Classic 15×15 board with 4 goties per corner, 3D perspective tumbling dice cube, and live emoji toasts
